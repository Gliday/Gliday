## Hi, I'm Gliday Yuka Luvonga 👋

**Conservation technology & bioinformatics researcher** based in Nairobi, Kenya.

I work where field ecology meets data science: modernising aerial wildlife surveys with camera-based counts and AI-assisted image processing, and applying genomics and bioinformatics to research questions in conservation and human health.

  Leading Aerial Survey Reform: oblique camera counts (OCC), terabyte-scale aerial imagery pipelines, benchmarking AI annotation platforms, and human-in-the-loop annotation workflows.
- **Founder & Research Lead, Shamba AI** (2024–present)
  Exploring AI-enabled, climate-smart decision support for smallholder farmers in East Africa.
